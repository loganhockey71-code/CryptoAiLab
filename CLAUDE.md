# CLAUDE.md — Project Rules, Architecture & Execution Blueprint

## Architecture & Hard Constraints
- Local Deployment: Strictly at http://localhost:4000. DO NOT create Vercel or cloud deployment files.
- Single Environment File: Read credentials ONLY from one `.env` file in the project root. Never create `.env.local` or `.env.example`.
- Hard Safety Switch: Explicit `PAPER_TRADING=true` required. Strictly paper trading with $2,000 USD virtual capital. Live order-placement functions are forbidden. Read-only exchange data allowed.
- Dynamic Universe (funnel): Stage 0 RADAR sweeps every coin CoinGecko lists (~8,500) every 4h on cheap data only (rate-limit paced, resumes after a 429); tradable coins are refreshed far more often with batched calls (shortlist + positions every 10 min, the rest every 60 min) and a coin seen ripping is promoted to analysis immediately; the 5-minute scan never waits for the sweep. Coinbase order-book spread/depth is context for the Research Brain only (price, market cap, volume, 1h/24h/7d change, volume anomalies). A coin is tradable if it has an online Coinbase USD market (preferred: WebSocket feed, deepest books) OR a Gate.io USDT spot market (public read-only REST: candles, tickers polled every 5s, order book; 1m candles polled every 15s for coins held or pending; leveraged tokens excluded; a same-ticker coin priced >30% differently on Gate is treated as a different asset and not mapped); stablecoins and wrapped/staked tokens excluded. Only coins on neither venue are not tradable (no price source), and illiquid coins are still rejected by the liquidity screens. Binance is geo-blocked (HTTP 451) from this machine and can be added as another venue later. Stage 1 SHORTLIST (150, range 100-300) of the highest cheap-priority tradable coins gets candle analysis every scan; the rest every 3rd scan and on first sight, so any coin can move up. Stage 2 DEEP RESEARCH (20, range 10-30) of the strongest candidates only: derivatives, news, macro, smart money, history and the AI. No Top-100 rank or trending flag is needed. Priority never lowers a requirement. Illiquid (< $1M 24h volume), tiny (< $5M cap), wash-trading-looking, thin-volume-spike and insufficient-data coins are rejected. CoinGecko primary, CoinMarketCap validates the top 300.

## Dual-Brain Architecture & Data Hierarchy
- Data Hierarchy: Direct exchange feed > Specialized derivatives (Coinglass) > Official macro/gov (FRED/Congress) > Reputable news & on-chain. Timestamp and provenance required for all signals. No duplicate counting across aggregators.
- The Brain (`src/brain.js`, `src/structure.js`, `src/newsimpact.js`, `src/learning.js`) is the decision-maker: a deterministic, pure engine that reads market structure (swings, HH/HL vs LH/LL, support/resistance, ranges, breakouts, retests, fake breakouts / liquidity sweeps), supply/demand (volume-weighted buyer vs seller pressure, supply<->demand shifts, absorption), candlestick patterns and rejection, volume, momentum, BTC/ETH regime, news impact and risk/reward on 1d/4h/1h/15m/5m, and outputs BUY / WATCH / IGNORE (flat) or HOLD / SELL (open position), classifies every coin HOT / WATCH / NEUTRAL / AVOID / IGNORE, and explains each decision from the inputs it actually used. It never buys inside a 1h/4h downtrend without a confirmed structure break, never from one signal, and waits (WATCH) with a stated trigger when there is no edge.
- Entry quality (`brain.js`): every coin gets LONG and SHORT evaluations built from three questions that are BLENDED into one ranking composite (not three separate cutoffs): (1) DIRECTION (structure, supply/demand, relative strength vs BTC, ETH and the market, ONE momentum family, regime/news context), (2) TIMING (anti-chasing: move already made in ATRs, distance from the breakout/retest level and swing, spent volume spike, stretch, share of the move used; a trigger, or several weaker confirmations together), (3) TRADE GEOMETRY (structural stop, realistic target, net R:R after fees + spread + slippage, empirical probability, EV). A very weak part and chasing pull the composite down. Preferred sequence: breakout -> confirmation -> retest -> entry, but it is a preference. Every decision carries a "Why NOW" answer; no trigger at all = WAIT.
- Ranking funnel: all coins -> liquidity (24h volume >= $1M, market cap >= $5M, a missing market cap is fine when volume >= $5M) -> quality screens (extreme volume/market-cap, a 20%+ thin-volume hour and price disagreements are WARNINGS that cost score, not rejections) -> relative strength vs BTC, ETH and the market (reward coins STARTING strong, penalise ones already up 20%+) -> shortlist -> deep analysis -> setups -> eligible coins are RANKED against each other each scan and the best are taken.
- Research LLM (optional): may only DOWNGRADE a Brain BUY to WATCH (`research.reviewDecision`), with a stated reason. It cannot create a BUY, change a stop/target or lift a veto; an outage never rejects a setup.
- Execution Engine: Minimal local latency evaluation upon receiving live 1m market ticks without waiting for LLM API calls.
- Continuous Loop: Scan -> Validate -> Analyze -> Candidate Signal -> Candle Confirm -> Risk Filter -> Enter -> Monitor -> Trail Profit -> Exit -> Calculate Net P&L -> Post-Mortem -> Update Lessons -> Resume.

## Risk Management & Safety Limits
- Default State: 100% Cash (Long-only for v1: SHORT setups are detected, scored, journaled and measured as counterfactuals, but `config.brain.allowShortTrades=false` keeps them unexecuted). 1x only, never any leverage. Holding time is flexible (minutes, hours or 1-3 days) while the thesis remains valid.
- Entry Confirmation: Candle confirmation required before entering any setup. Price freshness: candidate evaluation accepts a price up to 30s old; immediately before any paper entry the price is re-fetched and must be <= 10s old and within 0.5% of the confirmed price, otherwise the entry is rejected. An AI outage/formatting failure never rejects a setup: models are retried (with stricter JSON instructions) and the coin is re-tried later. Confirmation (rules 59-68): immediately before entry the setup, R:R, scores, order book and BTC regime are RECALCULATED from fresh candles, book and BTC; the 1m confirmation must confirm the thesis: a bullish-ish close (green, or closing in the upper half of its range) in or just past the entry zone, holding the level, on reasonable volume (>= 0.6x average), without violating the stop; it need not be a perfect green candle. A setup is not discarded after exactly 3 candles: when the window ends it is reassessed once if the structure is intact and price is still in the zone. If price moves materially away from the planned entry (beyond the zone by 1 ATR) the entry is cancelled and recalculated instead of chased; a price only slightly past the zone recalculates the zone around the live price.
- Position Sizing: risk-based, no minimum size. `size_usd = (equity x 1%) / (stop% + estimated fees+slippage%)`, never above the 30% per-position cap; max 3 active positions. A stopped-out trade loses at most ~1% of equity including costs.
- Risk & Exit: Mandatory stop-loss by coin: BTC 1.5-2.0%, ETH 2.0-2.5%, Top 20 2.5-3.0%, Top 21-50 3-4%, Top 51-100 and memes 4%. NO stop is ever wider than 4% on any pair or chain, and a stop is never widened after entry. Take-profit zone by tier: BTC 4-6%, ETH 5-8%, Top 20 6-10%, Top 21-50 8-15%, Top 51-100 10-20%, memes 10-25%+; reaching it banks a 50% partial and moves the stop to breakeven (tighten only). The remainder runs on a Chandelier trailing stop (highest high - k x ATR(15m), k = 3.0 / 2.5 / 2.0 / 1.5 as profit passes 0 / 1R / 2R / 3R, tighter on fading momentum or a non-bullish BTC regime). `downtrend_exit` cuts a LOSING own long (0.3R or more underwater, never at/over the stop) when 3 straight 5m bars make lower highs and lower lows below the 5m EMA21 with RSI < 40 and falling and no bounce, confirmed over 3 consecutive 1m closes (swing holds also need a broken 1h trend); it only exits earlier than the stop, never adds risk, and also starts the 2h asset cooldown. `momentum_reversal` needs structure (2 consecutive lower 1m lows on declining volume, or 2 closes below the 21 EMA with RSI < 45); one red candle never exits a winner. Targets are never forced. Holding 1-2 days is normal: the Research Brain picks a 1-48h horizon, and any position held more than ~6h (the default) ignores 1-minute noise: no `momentum_reversal`/`regime_exit`, a wider 1h-ATR Chandelier trail, and it only exits early on a broken 1h trend (after 1h open) or its stop/trail. BTC/ETH/market regime is a MODIFIER, not a switch: the BTC trend influences every long (weak BTC = lower probability, a higher composite floor, 0.6x size; strong BTC = a small confidence boost), a coin with exceptional relative strength is still judged on its own merits, and only a SEVERE market (BTC in a strong 4h AND 1h downtrend, or -5% in 6h / -8% in 24h) blocks new longs. Min R:R ratio >= 2.5:1 (protected, measured net of fees, spread and slippage). ENTRY ELIGIBILITY: composite (direction/timing/geometry) above a floor (55, +4/+8 in a weak BTC regime, -3 in a strong one, -4 for exceptional relative strength) AND EV meaningfully positive (>= 0.05R and still > 0 after subtracting one standard error of the estimate, so thin data cannot pass) AND protected R:R/stop rules AND no real veto AND some trigger. REAL VETOES ONLY: severe market; catastrophic coin-specific news (hack, insolvency, delisting, regulator action); a strong 4h downtrend or a 1h downtrend with heavy selling and NO reversal evidence; spread > 0.4% or an extremely thin book; too little volatility to cover fees, spread, stop and target; a structural stop wider than 4%. Everything else (downtrends, sellers in control, several bearish signals, 15m downtrend, mid-range, chasing, outside the zone, single evidence family, normal negative news, odd volume) is a score PENALTY or caution. A strong direction with a bad entry price ends up WAIT because timing drags the composite down. Until 30+ own trades are measured with a positive average R every entry risks only HALF of the normal 1% (smaller than the cap, never larger).
- Circuit Breakers: -5% daily equity loss halts trading for the day. 3 consecutive loss days freeze execution until manual review. 3 single-day losses or 2 losses in 60 mins force a 3-hour freeze. 2-hour lockout on stop-loss hits.
- Execution Realism: Subtract 0.10% fee and 0.05% slippage per trade.

## Structured Self-Learning (Wins & Losses)
- Every notable decision (BUY and the strongest WATCH / vetoed setups) is journaled with the evidence that produced it (`logs/brain-journal.json`); its outcome is measured later from real candles (MFE / MAE, target-or-stop-first), traded or not (counterfactual). Rules are learned per FEATURE BUCKET, never per trade/coin, and go live only after walk-forward validation (same sign on an earlier AND a later slice of time, 8+/5+ samples, >= 0.1R). They move the score by at most +-8 points and never touch risk limits, circuit breakers, stops, sizing or vetoes. `learning.findMissed` answers "a coin went up X%: why was it not caught?" from what the AI said before the move.
- Empirical probabilities (`src/empirical.js`): P(price reaches +R before the stop) per setup and direction from measured walk-forward observations of the population the Brain would ACTUALLY take (candidates passing every gate except the EV test, so the estimate is not blind to adverse selection: calibrating on all setups made a looser rule set take 74 entries at -0.28R in the 30-day backtest) (`npm run calibrate` writes `logs/calibration.json`), shrunk toward a zero-drift prior (no claimed edge), with an out-of-sample haircut (actual/predicted on unseen data). The live journal adds its own measured observations. Targets must be realistic for the setup's measured reach (MFE/MAE, max R, time to target/stop are recorded); a 2.5R target on a setup that rarely reaches it is flagged and fails geometry. Predicted-vs-actual with sample sizes is shown in the Learning Report.
- Setups: pullback, breakout + retest, fresh/range breakout, early reversal, liquidity sweep + reclaim, momentum continuation, relative-strength move, support bounce, trend continuation. A setup does not need every condition to be perfect (one or two disagreeing indicators are fine); volume is compared with the coin's OWN normal (1.2x), not a fixed multiple. NEW setups are mined from history (`npm run discover`, `src/discovery.js`): a feature combination becomes a 'discovered' setup only if it beats the same-hour market after costs on UNSEEN data (t >= 3, positive in 3 of 4 time chunks); the latest run found none, which is a valid result.
- Probability and EV: probabilities come from measured outcomes of comparable setups and are labelled with their sample size and source; below 30 measured cases confidence is reduced (an uncertainty allowance of one standard error is subtracted from EV), never invented. EV = P(target) x net reward - P(stop) x net loss, net of fees, slippage AND spread.
- EXPLORATION MODE (paper only, `config.brain.explore`): a setup that fails ONLY the protected 2.5R requirement (every other gate passed: no real veto, composite floor, a why-now, net R:R >= 1.5; only the EV test is waived because EV is computed at the lower R:R, so it is the same shortfall) may be traded at TINY risk, 0.10%-0.25% of equity (higher composite = more, never above the 1% cap; size = equity x risk% / (stop% + costs)). At most 2 exploration positions, always leaving one position slot free; the 3-position cap, 4% stop, circuit breakers, cooldown, fresh-price and every other rule still apply. The protected 2.5R minimum for NORMAL trades is unchanged. Outcomes are tracked separately (mode 'exploration', realised R = net P&L / dollars risked) with the average R, a 95% interval and R:R buckets; nothing is concluded before 50 closed trades, 'decisive' only at 100, and 'positive' means the LOWER bound is above zero. Exploration pauses itself if its closed trades lose 2% of starting capital, or if 50+ trades show negative expectancy. The report never changes a rule: lowering the normal minimum is a human decision made from this evidence. SECOND EXPERIMENT (score): a setup whose composite is under the normal floor (55) but at least 5 points lower (50) while the full 2.5R requirement IS met is also traded at the same tiny risk (EV test waived, every other gate and the 2.5R rule apply). The two experiments are never combined (a setup failing both is not eligible) and are reported separately: win rate, average R with 95% interval, fees+slippage in R, modelled EV, and score buckets 50-52 / 52-54.5. The normal floor of 55 is lowered only by a human decision after that evidence is positive.
- Counterfactuals: every WAIT, vetoed setup AND declined coin (plain 1.5 ATR / 2.5R plan) is journaled and measured. Loss-cluster analysis tags repeated causes of losses (late entry, bad regime, low liquidity, breakout failure, unrealistic target, excess pre-entry move, weak timing, setup type) with lift and persistence across both halves of the record; clusters are hypotheses for a human and never change a rule automatically. A single loss never changes anything.
- News reaction ledger: event -> expected direction -> affected assets -> actual reaction (1h/4h/24h, magnitude in ATRs, delay). A headline type gets full weight only after 15+ measured reactions agree out of sample; until then it counts at 0.5x. A reaction is a measurement, not proof of causation.
- Honest status: `scripts/backtest-brain.mjs` and `scripts/research-long.mjs` (240 days x 38 coins) found NO statistically validated, cost-beating edge in trend / momentum / structure signals; short-term strength mean-reverts and buying oversold downtrends underperforms. The Brain's probabilities are therefore priors anchored to measured base rates, labelled unvalidated until 60+ outcomes are measured.
- Mandatory post-mortem reflection after EVERY trade (both winning and losing trades).
- Track recurring patterns and adjust future signal confidence based on historical results in Supabase.
- The self-learning system must NEVER automatically alter core risk parameters or circuit breakers.

## Copy Trading (Hyperliquid, paper only) — DISABLED AS A TRADING SOURCE
- `config.copy.mirror = false`: tracked traders are a DATA input only (1% weight in the Brain score, +2 radar points). They can never open, add to, size or veto a position; `openCopyPosition` refuses. Existing legacy copy positions are still exited with their leader / stop. The rules below describe how traders are scored and are kept for the data feed and for legacy positions.
- Sources: Hyperliquid public leaderboard + info/WebSocket API (read-only, no keys, no order/signing endpoints). Zerion (on-chain wallets, key in `.env` as `ZERION_API_KEY`) is the second source; both run at the same time and share the same rules.
- Win-rate floor is HARD: only traders with a verified win rate >= 75% are ever tracked; >= 80% ranks first. A "trade" is one closing order (fills grouped by order/TWAP id) net of fees.
- Extra qualification (protects against inflated win rates): >= 30 closed trades, >= 7 days of history on >= 5 active days, profit factor >= 1.5, positive net P&L, not bot-like (<= 40 closes/day), active in the last 14 days, account >= $10k, open positions not underwater by more than 10% of the account.
- Mirroring: entries, adds, partial exits, full exits and flips follow the leader. Size = leader's % of their account applied to our equity, capped at 30%. Skip if our price is more than 1% worse than the leader's fill, or the fill is more than 120s old (exits are never skipped for age).
- Applies to copies: circuit breakers, shared max 3 open positions, 30% size cap, fresh-data check (10s), fees + slippage, 2h asset cooldown after a stop, hard 4% protective stop (may be set wider for a specific trade, absolute maximum 7%), post-mortem after every trade. Shorts are allowed for copied trades (paper, 1x, no leverage). The BTC-regime / confluence / R:R filters apply to the LLM strategy only.

## Per-Trader Loss-Streak Ladder (all copy sources)
Counts consecutive losing copies of the SAME trader; any win resets it. Exits/mirrored closes are never blocked, only new entries and adds.
- 1 loss: keep copying. 2 in a row: status PAUSED for 3h and an immediate re-score. 3 in a row: pause 24h and re-score the trader (must still qualify). 4 in a row: stop copying until manual review. 5+: removed from the active pool (same manual release needed).
- Derived from closed trades in Supabase, so it survives restarts. Operator release: dashboard "release" button (`POST /api/traders/release`), which clears the streak and forces a re-score.
- Thresholds live in `config.copy.loserStreak`; it is a risk rule, so self-learning must never change it.

## On-chain Copy Trading (Zerion, paper only)
- Discovery: GeckoTerminal trending pools on eth/base/arbitrum/bsc/solana -> wallets trading them (plus any in `ZERION_WATCH_WALLETS`). Scoring + live detection: Zerion decoded swaps. Prices/liquidity: DexScreener.
- A "trade" is one SELL of a non-stable, non-major token whose purchase was seen in the window (average-cost USD P&L net of gas). Sells without a known basis (airdrops, pre-window buys) are ignored, never counted as wins.
- Same qualification as Hyperliquid (win rate >= 75% hard floor, >= 80% preferred, plus trade count, history, profit factor, bot and underwater checks).
- Copy only into tokens we could exit: liquidity >= $200k, 24h volume >= $100k, pair age >= 3 days. Long-only (spot). Skip entries detected more than 300s late or more than 1% worse than the leader's price; exits are never skipped for age.
- Same position cap (max 3 across ALL sources), 30% size cap, circuit breakers, 4% hard stop, fees + slippage and post-mortems as every other trade.

## News, Politics & Regulation (read every scan, shown in the News & Politics panel)
- Tier 3 (official): White House presidential actions, Federal Register executive orders, Federal Reserve press releases + speeches, SEC and CFTC press releases, Congress.gov bills (crypto, tariffs, sanctions, securities, banking vocabulary), FRED macro series.
- Tier 4 (news / unofficial): CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, BBC World, NPR Politics, CNBC Markets, Google News (tariffs/sanctions/Fed/executive orders/war/election), and an UNOFFICIAL Truth Social mirror (trumpstruth.org). X/Twitter is not covered (no free API).
- Political and news items are read by `newsimpact.js` into structured events (what happened -> affected coins -> bullish / bearish / uncertain -> strength -> duration -> priced in or not) that feed the Brain's score and its dangerous-news veto. Headlines that deny, doubt or speculate are 'uncertain' and never veto; one unofficial market-wide headline can never veto the whole market. They never trigger a trade by themselves, and copy trading ignores them.

## Immutable Guardrail Layer (`src/guardrails.js`)
Every entry (strategy and copy) goes through `guard.finalizeEntry()` and every stop change through `guard.keepStopTight()`. The module's limits are deep-frozen copies of `config.risk`; strategy code can propose stops/sizes but cannot exceed the 1% risk cap, the 4% stop cap, the 30% position cap, or the circuit breakers. Self-learning never touches it.
